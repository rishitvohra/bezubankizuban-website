About us

Every dog on the street has a story.
We make sure it doesn't end in silence.

Bezubankizuban was founded by a small group of neighbourhood volunteers who kept running into the same problem: injured dogs with nowhere to go, litters born onto the street with no one tracking them, and communities that cared but didn't know how to help. We built the systems — rescue routes, a vet network, feeding rosters, and an adoption pipeline — so that care isn't occasional. It's routine.

We work directly with local veterinarians, municipal bodies, and residents' associations, and every rupee donated goes toward medicine, food, sterilization, and shelter — not overhead.

"A city can be measured by how it treats the animals who have no one to speak for them."

— Our founding note, 2019

Our agenda

Six commitments. No shortcuts.

Rescue & Rehabilitation

Round-the-clock response for injured, sick, and abandoned dogs, with shelter and recovery care until they're fit to return or be adopted.

Sterilization (ABC)

Humane Animal Birth Control drives with local vets to stabilise street populations and prevent the cruelty that comes with overcrowding.

Vaccination & Healthcare

Anti-rabies and routine vaccination camps, deworming, and free treatment for community dogs across our coverage area.

Daily Feeding Program

Scheduled feeding routes across neighbourhoods, run by trained volunteers, so no dog goes hungry — rain or shine.

Adoption & Rehoming

We vet loving homes and support every adopter with vaccination records, training guidance, and follow-up visits.

Awareness & Education

School talks, volunteer drives, and neighbourhood workshops that build a kinder, dog-safe city over time.
